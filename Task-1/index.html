Who We Are

InAmigos Foundation started in September 2020 with a simple belief: real change happens when communities come together. Founded by Govind Shukla in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, we set out to bridge the massive gaps in education, healthcare, and basic economic opportunities for those who need it most.

We don't just want to offer temporary relief. Whether we are setting up digital literacy camps for kids, empowering women through skill-building, or rescuing stray animals, our goal is long-term empowerment. We are a registered Section 8 non-profit, recognized by NITI Aayog, and we run entirely on the passion of our volunteers and partners.

We believe in grassroot action. When you unite minds, you can truly change the world.
